The Bid Gym

Every bot at this table plays identical cards — gen23 plays every trick, every go-down, every trump call. The only thing that varies is the auction, in the family's −250/+500 format where one bad contract is a cliff. Three acts so far: the gene cities, the AlphaBid oracle, and the partnership league now running — locked pairs evolving their own bidding language against the gen23 & gen23 house team.

Act III — the partnership league

Locked pairs for life. Each partner carries 13 interpretable genes that modulate gen23's own Q-values — all-zero genes bid byte-identically to the house, so every surviving deviation is a discovery. Fitness is mirrored-deck point differential (EMA-smoothed); every 8th season the league runs the gauntlet against the locked gen23 & gen23 house team.

Act II — the oracle saga

AlphaBid: sample worlds consistent with the auction, roll each out with frozen gen23, wager in win-probability currency. The sweep proved listening matters and worlds matter — and found the wall.

Act I — the gene cities (retired)

Each league: 10 evolving genomes and two immortal anchors — family (the calibrated family bidder, score-blind) and gen23 (the net's own neural auction). Every season is a duplicate-deck round-robin; the bottom of the table is culled and rebred from the top. The gene fingerprint shows how far each champion has drifted from the family bidder.

Gene drift — what is evolution deciding?

Champion gene values by season, one line per city; the dashed line is the family default. Watch logan (cold start from random genes): if it converges to the others, bidding wisdom is being rediscovered, not inherited.

The poker question — do they bid the score?

Average winning bid when the bidder's team was behind vs ahead at the deal, latest season. The family bidder cannot tell the difference by construction — any gap it shows is deal noise; a champion with a real gap has learned to bid the situation, not just the hand.

World cup

City champions on fresh salted decks nobody trained on, plus both anchors. League standings are weather; this is climate.

Same hand, different situation

The curiosity instrument: a fixed panel of dealt hands is shown to every bidder under 7 auction scenarios × 9 score states, and the exact bid is recorded. Pick a bidder and a hand — the grid is its complete behavioral X-ray for that hand. Darker means a higher bid.
